Request Assistance

This request form is for authorized representatives of Lipa City LGUs only. KBK does not accept individual or family assistance requests. Approval is not guaranteed; an administrator will review the information.

Before you submit: KBK uses the information in this form to review and follow up on Lipa City LGU requests. Do not include passwords, financial account details, or unnecessary medical details.
