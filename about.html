OUR NAME, OUR PURPOSE

What Does KBK Stand For?

KBK stands for Kapisanan Buklod ng Kabataan, which means an association that brings young people together. KBK is a youth-led community organization founded on the spirit of bayanihan and pagkakaisa.

Our name embodies our mission: to bring young people together in service, compassion, and meaningful action for Lipa City. KBK coordinates community support through Local Government Units (LGUs) in Lipa City only.

Our Purpose

We exist to make giving easier, more transparent, and more meaningful. Through this Donation Management System, every peso, item, and act of kindness is recorded, tracked, and allocated so donors can see how their generosity supports Lipa City LGUs.
