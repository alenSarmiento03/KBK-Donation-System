About our organization

Kapisanan Buklod ng Kabataan (KBK)

Making giving easier, more transparent, and more meaningful.

What Does KBK Stand For?

KBK stands for Kapisanan Buklod ng Kabataan, which means an association that brings young people together. KBK is a youth-led community organization founded on the spirit of bayanihan and pagkakaisa.

Our name embodies our mission: to bring young people together in service, compassion, and meaningful action for the community.

K

We are a collective of individuals united by a shared vision of service and compassion.

B

Bond / Unity

We stand together as one, bound by respect, trust, and the common goal of helping those in need.

We are the youth: the driving force of change, hope, and progress in our community.

Our Purpose

We exist to make giving easier, more transparent, and more meaningful. Through this Donation Management System, every peso, item, and act of kindness is recorded, tracked, and allocated so donors can see how their generosity supports the community.

Isang Buklod. Isang Bayanihan. Isang Layunin.One Bond. One Community Spirit. One Goal.

Our Mission

To unite young people in compassionate community service and make giving easier, more transparent, and more meaningful.

Our Vision

A united and compassionate community where generosity creates meaningful, visible impact for those in need.

Our Goals

Encourage bayanihan

Bring young people and community members together to support people in need.

Make giving transparent

Help donors understand how contributions are recorded, tracked, and allocated.

Deliver meaningful support

Connect donated resources with community needs through responsible coordination.
