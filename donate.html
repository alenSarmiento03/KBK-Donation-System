    Become a Donor

    Thank you for supporting KBK and our community. Contact KBK before donating so the team can confirm current needs and the safest hand-off method.

    Cash donation

    Please contact KBK to coordinate an in-person donation and receive an official acknowledgement. We do not publish bank or wallet details on this demo site.

    In-kind donation

    Food, school supplies, hygiene items, and other requested essentials may be accepted based on current needs. Please confirm item suitability and condition before arranging delivery.

    Donation drop-off

    Drop-offs should be arranged in advance with a KBK representative. Ask for the confirmed location, schedule, and receipt process before travelling.
